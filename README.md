# HR-Analytics-Dashboard
Aim : To develop an interactive Power BI HR Analytics Dashboard that provides comprehensive insights into workforce performance, employee demographics, and HR metrics, enabling data-driven decision-making to improve productivity, retention, attrition and organizational efficiency.

Objective : To design and implement an interactive Power BI HR Analytics Dashboard that consolidates workforce data, including age and education demographics, to provide actionable insights on employee distribution, recruitment, retention, and attrition. The dashboard will help HR managers make data-driven decisions for talent management, succession planning, and skill development, while promoting diversity, inclusion, and organizational growth.


Key Feature:
1) Employee Demographics Analysis – Age, gender, department, location, and role distribution.
2) Workforce Strength Tracking – Total employees, hires, exits
3) Attrition & Retention Metrics – Voluntary and involuntary turnover rates with trend analysis.
4) Filter & Drill-Down Options – By department, job role, job satisfaction.
5) Age Distribution – Visualize employees by age groups (e.g., 20–30, 31–40, 41–50, 50+).
6) Education vs. Role – Match qualifications with job positions.
