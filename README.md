# HR-Analytics-Dashboard 

To develop an interactive Power BI HR Analytics Dashboard that provides comprehensive insights into workforce performance, employee demographics, and HR metrics, enabling data-driven decision-making to improve productivity, retention, attrition and organizational efficiency.

# Objective :-
1. To design and implement an interactive Power BI HR Analytics Dashboard that consolidates workforce data, including age and education demographics, to provide actionable insights on employee distribution, recruitment, retention, and attrition. 
2. The dashboard will help HR managers make data-driven decisions for talent management, succession planning, and skill development, while promoting diversity, inclusion, and organizational growth.


# Key Feature :-

1. Employee Demographics Analysis – Age, gender, department, location, and role distribution.
2. Workforce Strength Tracking – Total employees, hires, exits
3. Attrition & Retention Metrics – Voluntary and involuntary turnover rates with trend analysis.
4. Filter & Drill-Down Options – By department, job role, job satisfaction.
5. Age Distribution – Visualize employees by age groups (e.g., 20–30, 31–40, 41–50, 50+).
6. Education vs. Role – Match qualifications with job positions.

# Result :- 
1 . Successfully developed an interactive dashboard in Microsoft Power BI to analyze HR data with dynamic visualizations and KPI tracking.
2. The dashboard enabled clear visualization of workforce strength, employee demographics, attrition trends, and retention performance across multiple departments.
3. HR teams can now make faster and more accurate decisions related to hiring, employee development, and workforce optimization through real-time analytical insights.
4. The project demonstrated how HR analytics can improve organizational productivity, employee retention, and strategic planning by transforming raw HR data into actionable information.

# Conclusion :- 

1. The HR Analytics Dashboard provides a centralized view of employee demographics, workforce distribution, and HR performance metrics, helping organizations monitor workforce health effectively.
2. Interactive filters and drill-down analysis enable HR managers to identify trends in attrition, retention, recruitment, and employee satisfaction across departments and job roles.
3. Age distribution and education-versus-role insights support better talent management, succession planning, diversity initiatives, and skill development strategies.
4. By using data-driven insights, organizations can improve employee engagement, reduce turnover, optimize workforce planning, and enhance overall organizational efficiency.
